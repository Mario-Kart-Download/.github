## Key Features of Mario Kart 8 Deluxe

### 2. Exciting Racing Tracks

The game includes a wide variety of racing courses with different themes, environments, and challenges. Players can race through beautiful cities, underwater locations, colorful forests, dangerous castles, and futuristic environments. ### 3. Vehicle Customization

Players can customize their racing vehicles by selecting different kart bodies, tires, and gliders. Each combination offers different performance characteristics, including speed, acceleration, handling, and traction. Finding the right vehicle setup can make a noticeable difference during competitive races.

### 4. Fun Racing Items

Items are an important part of Mario Kart 8 Deluxe. Players can collect special objects during races and use them to gain an advantage or slow down their opponents.

Some popular items include:

- Red Shell: Targets opponents ahead of you.
- Banana: Creates obstacles for other racers.
- Mushroom: Provides a temporary speed boost.
- Blue Shell: Targets the racer in first place.
- Star: Provides temporary invincibility and increased speed.
- Boo: Makes the player temporarily invisible and can steal an opponent's item.

Players can also carry two items at once, creating additional racing strategies.&#x20;

### 5. Multiplayer Racing

Multiplayer is one of the biggest attractions of Mario Kart 8 Deluxe. Players can compete with friends and family using local multiplayer or challenge other racers through online modes. The game supports up to four players on one system, eight through local wireless, and twelve online.&#x20;

Apart from traditional racing, the game features several battle modes where players compete using special rules and objectives. Balloon Battle, Bob omb Blast, Coin Runners, Shine Thief, and Renegade Roundup provide different ways to enjoy the game.

### 7. Beginner Friendly Controls

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe is designed for players with different skill levels. Its simple controls make it easy for beginners to start racing, while advanced techniques such as drifting, boosting, and selecting effective kart combinations provide additional challenges for experienced players.

Smart Steering is also available to help beginners stay on the track while learning the gameplay.

### 8. Beautiful Graphics and Sound Effects

The game features colorful environments, detailed character animations, impressive racing effects, and entertaining background music. Each track has its own visual identity, making the racing experience enjoyable throughout different cups and competitions.
